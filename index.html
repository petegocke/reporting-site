The problem

Most outsourced accounting firms want to offer more value. Most don’t have the time, tools, or frameworks to do it well.

In conversations with multiple accounting and fractional CFO teams, a consistent pattern emerged:

  • Firms want to move beyond basic financial statements
  • They want to offer “strategic” services
  • But day-to-day work is dominated by closing books, managing clients, and keeping systems clean

As a result, advisory promises often outpace delivery — not because of bad intent, but because there’s no shared reporting backbone to support it.

The moment it clicked

The issue isn’t expertise or effort. It’s structure.

Most CFO-style services rely on generic reports, inconsistent metrics, and tools that aren’t intuitive or industry-specific. That makes it hard to deliver real strategic value in a repeatable, honest way.

What firms need isn’t another platform. They need clear, standardized reporting that gives them a credible starting point for higher-value conversations.
